# Stop Keying In Invoices. Start Approving Them.

**For property management and accounts-payable teams buried in vendor bills.**

## The problem

Every month your AP team opens hundreds of PDFs, retypes vendor names, invoice numbers, amounts and due dates, chases approvals, then logs in to vendor portals one by one to pay. It's slow, it's error-prone, and one fat-fingered amount or double payment costs real money.

## What we do

Drop in an invoice — a clean PDF or a blurry scan — and the app takes it from there:

1. **Reads it for you.** A text parser handles clean PDFs; when that comes up short, an AI reader (Gemini) fills in the gaps from scans and images. It pulls vendor, property, invoice number, amount and due date, and leaves a field blank rather than guess.
2. **Checks it against your rules.** You set the limits on the Rules page: a maximum auto-approval amount, how far above a vendor's average a bill may run, and how much billing history a vendor needs. Invoices that pass for autopay vendors are approved on the spot. Everything else waits for a person, with the reasons it was held.
3. **Pays it.** For approved autopay invoices, an AI browser agent goes to the vendor's payment site and pays the bill. It keeps running in the background even after you close the tab.
4. **Proves it.** An invoice is marked paid only after the receipt's confirmation number and amount match. Failed or uncertain attempts are logged and stay open for review. Duplicate invoices and repeat payments are blocked.
5. **Keeps the record.** A live status banner, a progress bar for each payment and an Activity log of every change show your team what happened and why.

## Why teams choose it

- **Hours back every week.** Routine bills go from inbox to paid with no typing.
- **Control stays with you.** Your thresholds decide what gets automated, and anything unusual goes to a human.
- **No invented data.** Missing or ambiguous values are flagged, never filled in by a guess.
- **Audit-ready by default.** Every approval, rejection and payment attempt is recorded.
- **Safe failure.** When something can't be verified, the invoice stays open and the problem is visible.

## The bottom line

Your team stops being data entry and starts being the final word on what gets paid. Routine invoices go through automatically and the unusual ones reach a person.
